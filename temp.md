# Improve reasoning over time

## Goal
Make reasoning quality measurable and trackable over time.

## Baseline
For 3-5 recent reasoning episodes, record:
- claim clarity
- evidence quality
- uncertainty quality
- reversibility
- outcome quality

## Comparison template
For each important episode, record:
- what changed
- why it changed
- what evidence caused the change
- what got better
- what got worse

## Review loop
After each important episode, ask:
- Did the answer help?
- Was it auditable?
- Was it reversible?
- What should be done differently next time?

## Score
- reasoning quality score: 1-5
- confidence in score: low / medium / high

## Tracking rule
If the score trends up and failures decrease, reasoning is improving.
