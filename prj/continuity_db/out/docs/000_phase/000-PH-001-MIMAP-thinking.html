      • critical thinking
        • questions assumptions
        • checks evidence↗
        • detects fallacys
        • separates confidence from certainty
      • creative thinking
        • produces new possibilitys
        • combines distant ideas
        • uses analogy and imagination
        • tolerates ambiguity
      • analytical thinking
        • breaks wholes into parts
        • compares variables
        • traces causal chains
        • uses structured methods
      • systems thinking
        • sees relationships and feedback loops
        • studys incentives, delays, and side effects
        • avoids isolated local fixs
      • strategic thinking
        • links present choices to future outcomes
        • weighs constraints and opportunitys
        • plans sequences of moves
      • reflective thinking
        • observes one's own reasoning↗
        • reviews mistakes and successs
        • improves future judgment
      • practical thinking
        • adapts ideas to real constraints
        • prioritizs workable action
        • uses experience and context
    • cognitive processs
      • attention↗
        • selects what enters focus
        • filters distractions
        • sets priority
      • perception
        • interprets sensory or textual input
        • recognizs patterns
        • can be biased by expectations
      • memory
        • stores experiences and knowledge
        • retrieves relevant examples
        • supports comparison and prediction
      • abstraction
        • removes irrelevant detail
        • forms categorys and models
        • enables generalization
      • inference↗
        • draws conclusions from premiss
        • may be deductive, inductive, or abductive
      • evaluation
        • judges quality, truth, usefulness, or value
        • applys standards and criterions
      • metacognition
        • monitors thinking
        • asks whether the method is suitable
        • adjusts strategy when stuck
    • reasoning↗ modes
      • deductive reasoning↗
        • moves from general rules to necessary conclusions
        • valid when structure preserves truth
      • inductive reasoning↗
        • moves from examples to probable generalizations
        • strength depends on sample quality
      • abductive reasoning↗
        • chooss the best available explanation
        • common in diagnosis and investigation
      • analogical reasoning↗
        • transfers structure from a known case to a new case
        • useful but limited by hidden differences
      • probabilistic reasoning↗
        • handles uncertainty with likelihoods
        • updates confidence as evidence↗ changes

      • coherence
