## Requirement

Parenthesized morphology parts in MIMAP words shall become explicit HTML links while the plain stem remains visible as normal text, and all rendered parts shall preserve the source-token order.

## Details

- Parenthesized morphology parts in MIMAP words shall become explicit HTML links while the plain stem remains visible as normal text.
- Prefix links shall be rendered before the stem when the parenthesized part appears before the stem.
- Suffix links shall be rendered after the stem when the parenthesized part appears after the stem.
- Example: `ment(al)` shall render as stem text `ment` followed by an `al` link.
- Example: `(in)clude(s)` shall render as an `in` link, then stem text `clude`, then an `s` link.
- The rendered order shall always follow the source-token order: prefix link(s), stem text, suffix link(s).
