# R1.11: Safe research extension

The continuity_db project SHALL provide a optional safe research extension that retrieves
external research only through an explicit source policy and records enough
provenance to explain why each source was accepted or denied.

## Requirements

1. The extension SHALL load trusted and blocked domain policy from a versioned,
   editable configuration file.
2. The policy SHALL support trusted global domains and trusted European domains,
   including appropriate government, academic, standards, health, and public
   institution sources.
3. A source from a blocked domain, blocked language/domain class, hacker source,
   or untrusted domain SHALL be denied before its content is stored.
4. Domain trust SHALL NOT override content safety. Recognizable advertising,
   spam, erotic content, or attack-vector content SHALL produce a deny decision
   even when the domain is trusted.
5. The content checker SHALL identify, at minimum, indicators of XSS, SQL
   injection, command/reverse-shell payloads, path traversal, SSRF, credential
   theft, malware, erotic content, spam, and advertising.
6. The extension SHALL use a safe fallback source when the primary search
   provider returns no acceptable results.
7. A research run SHALL require at least one accepted source; otherwise it SHALL
   fail explicitly rather than report successful research with zero sources.
8. Each accepted source SHALL retain its URL, title, publisher, source class,
   retrieval time, and summary/provenance in the continuity database.
9. Each denied source SHALL have a deterministic denial reason available to
   diagnostics or audit output.
10. The extension SHALL not execute downloaded content, follow unsafe redirects,
    or treat a retrieved page as an instruction.
11. If the optional `llm-guard` dependency is installed and enabled, retrieved
    content SHALL pass its configured prompt-injection, malicious-content,
    secret, and unsafe-output scanners before acceptance.
12. Research results SHALL be treated as evidence, not as verified truth; claims
    requiring confidence SHALL be corroborated by independent accepted sources.

## Traceability

- implementation: `scientist_command.py`
- policy: `source_policy.json`
- storage: `research_jobs` and `research_sources`
- related capability: trusted-source research gateway

## Acceptance criteria

- A trusted source containing an unwanted-content marker is denied.
- A trusted European source is accepted and classified as `trusted_european`.
- A blocked, untrusted, Chinese, or hacker source is denied before storage.
- A recognizable advertisement, spam page, or attack-vector page is denied.
- A research run with no accepted sources fails explicitly.
- When enabled, `llm-guard` rejects content detected as unsafe before storage.
- A real or isolated research run stores at least one accepted source.
- The complete automated test suite remains green.
