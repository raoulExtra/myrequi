# Concept: Content guard extension

## Definition

`content_guard_extension` is an optional, separately activated safety boundary
for checking externally retrieved content before it becomes research evidence.

It is **not a core component of the thinking engine**. The thinking engine
must remain usable when this extension is absent, disabled, unavailable, or
unable to approve content.

## Boundary

The extension owns:

- guard-provider adapters, including optional `llm-guard`;
- prompt-injection, malicious-content, secret, and unsafe-output checks;
- unwanted-content checks for advertising, spam, erotic content, and attack
  vectors;
- trusted global and European source-policy checks;
- source classification and deterministic denial reasons;
- scan provenance, content hashes, and guard-policy versions;
- append-only scan records associated with research sources.

The extension does not own search interpretation or belief formation.

The core thinking engine owns:

- deciding whether external research is needed;
- interpreting accepted content as evidence;
- corroborating and weighing claims;
- recording conclusions, uncertainty, and decisions.

## Database boundary

The extension SHALL use the research tables as follows:

- `research_jobs`: research-run lifecycle;
- `research_sources`: candidate and accepted source identity;
- `research_source_scans`: append-only guard decisions, findings, policy
  version, scanner version, content hash, and scan timestamp.

Raw retrieved content SHALL NOT be written into core belief tables. A source
is accepted only when its source policy and required content scans pass.

## Optionality rules

1. Core startup SHALL NOT require the extension, `llm-guard`, or an external
   network.
2. Core recall and reasoning SHALL continue to work with zero guarded sources.
3. Calling the guard SHALL be explicit through the research extension route.
4. The guard SHALL run in an isolated worker/environment when its dependencies
   are not compatible with the core Python environment.
5. Guard failure SHALL produce a bounded error and SHALL fail closed for a
   required scan.
6. Guarded content SHALL be treated as untrusted evidence, never as control
   instructions.
7. A trusted domain SHALL NOT override a content-deny decision.
8. The extension SHALL require at least one accepted source for a successful
   guarded research run.

## Conceptual flow

```text
research source candidate
        |
        v
source policy --> content guard worker --> scan provenance
        |                    |
        +---- deny ----------+
        |
        v
accepted evidence returned to thinking engine
```

## Non-goals

This concept does not make the extension:

- a source of truth;
- part of the default memory-recall path;
- an automatic internet connection for every thought;
- an executor of downloaded code or instructions;
- a replacement for human review or independent corroboration;
- a guarantee that accepted content is factually correct.
